Hi! I’m Jerry, a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at **Stanford University** advised by [Prof. Leila Wehbe](https://leilawehbe.com/). Previously, I was a research assistant in the Machine Learning Department at **Carnegie Mellon University**. I hold an M.A. in Statistics from **UC Berkeley**, as well as a B.S. in Psychology and a B.A. in Philosophy from **Peking University**.

My general research interests lie in **Cognitive Computational Neuroscience**. Currently, I'm focusing on encoding models for MEG-fMRI fusion in naturalistic experiments. During undergrad, I've had the priviledge of working with [Prof. Huihui Zhang](https://www.ai.pku.edu.cn/en/info/1408/2098.htm) and [Prof. Huan Luo](https://mgv.pku.edu.cn/english/people/lbd/sopacs/360652.htm) on serial dependence. I also had a wonderful experience collaborating with [Prof. Michael Kahana](https://psychology.sas.upenn.edu/people/michael-kahana), where we developed CMR-IA, a model of memory for items and associations.
